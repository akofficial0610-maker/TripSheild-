🎯 Problem Statement

Every year millions of tourists face safety risks due to lack of real-time local safety information, language barriers, and unawareness of emergency services at their destination.

💡 Our Solution

TripShield is a unified tourist safety platform combining AI-powered guidance, real-time crowd-sourced risk mapping, and instant SOS capabilities — all in one place.

📍 Location-Aware

The app uses your device's GPS to detect your real-time location, display nearby risk zones, auto-fill local emergency numbers, and share coordinates in SOS situations.
